Common questions
Before you reach out.

Can we use just one pillar, or do we have to take everything?

Absolutely — most clients start with one pillar and expand over time. You might begin with Content Studio and add Digital Marketing six months later as your team sees the results. There's no minimum basket.

Do you work with startups or only established brands?

Both. We've worked with pre-revenue startups building their first brand presence and large multinationals needing a local-market partner. Budget and scope are calibrated to where you are.

What does onboarding look like?

Typically: a discovery session in week one, a strategy presentation at the end of week two, and first deliverables live by week four. For retainers, we embed deeply into your brand before producing anything.

Do you work with brands outside Kenya?

Yes — we have clients in Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda, and the wider African market. Our core expertise is East Africa, and we partner with local experts in markets we don't have a direct presence in.
