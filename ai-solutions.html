Common questions
Things people ask us first.

Do we need a large budget or big data set to start?

No. We've built effective AI systems for SMEs with modest data and modest budgets. The audit phase tells us exactly what's feasible with what you have — we never over-engineer.

Will the AI content sound like our brand?

Yes — that's a core part of our setup process. We train every system on your historical content, tone guidelines, and brand vocabulary before a single piece is published.

What happens if the AI gets something wrong?

Every automated output goes through a human review checkpoint before publishing. We design our pipelines with quality gates, not blind automation.

How long before we see results?

Most clients see measurable improvements in output volume and engagement within the first 4–6 weeks of a live pipeline. Campaign ROI improvements typically emerge by month 2–3.
